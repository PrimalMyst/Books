## Purpose
This file explains, in-world, why a player may hold multiple vessels in a season, and why only one vessel can be actively inhabited at a time.

## In-World Framing
- The player is a preserved consciousness, not a fixed body.
- Myrielle enables lawful vessel acquisition through sanctuary rites at the start of a season.
- Each acquired vessel is a valid anchor for the same consciousness within that season's reality instance.

## Why Up To Six Vessels
- Seasonal reality can sustain only a limited number of stable anchors per preserved consciousness.
- Myrielle's rite establishes up to six attuned anchor signatures before instability risk increases.
- This cap is a stability boundary, not a measure of personal worth.

## Why Only One Active Vessel
- A single consciousness cannot safely split into concurrent full-control streams inside one unstable instance.
- Full inhabitation requires complete attunement focus to one anchor body.
- Attempting simultaneous inhabitation would degrade memory integrity and threaten preservation continuity.

## Practical Fantasy Interpretation
- Creating a new character is Myrielle helping establish another attuned vessel.
- Switching characters is the consciousness re-attuning from one vessel to another.
- Re-attunement can be performed whenever the player chooses.

## Shared Fate, Personal Path
- Some seasonal achievements and world events are recorded at consciousness level and apply across all attuned vessels in that season.
- Other progress remains bound to the currently lived vessel (build, equipment, personal quest path).
- This keeps continuity of the preserved self while preserving each vessel's individual journey.

## Shared Stash and Hideout (Diegetic Framing)
- The common stash represents a preserved memory vault keyed to the season's attunement signature.
- All attuned vessels can retrieve from and deposit into the same vault within that season realm.
- The hideout is a single sanctuary anchor maintained by the preserved consciousness, regardless of which vessel is currently active.
- Inactive attuned vessels are preserved in the hideout as visible resting forms, showing the season's full living roster.
- In-world, vessel switching changes who walks the world, not ownership of the sanctuary or vault.

## Soul Fingerprint Authority
- Myrielle marks each preserved consciousness with a unique soul fingerprint, called the Light of Myrielle.
- The hideout recognizes this fingerprint rather than the currently inhabited body.
- As a result, any active vessel attuned to that consciousness can administer the same hideout.
