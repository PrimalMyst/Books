## Purpose
This file explains, in-world, why seasons reset and why characters transition to a persistent realm.

## In-World Framing
- Each active season is a finite reality instance under stress from divine conflict.
- As conflict intensifies, the instance reaches a terminal state and can no longer sustain continuity.
- The end of a season is the collapse point of that instance, not a simple administrative reset.

## Why Progress Can Continue
- Myrielle's preservation path allows worthy consciousness to cross instance boundaries.
- Valhalla acts as a sanctuary bridge during instance collapse.
- Preserved consciousness emerges in the Eternal Realm, which remains stable across cycles.

## Why Some Things Do Not Carry Over
- Season-only phenomena are tied to the collapsing instance itself.
- Temporary boons and currencies decay with the destroyed instance.
- Personal growth and preserved identity continue; local anomalies do not.

## Player-Facing Summary
- Seasons are worlds with a beginning and an end.
- Completion grants preservation and continuity.
- The Eternal Realm is continuity space, not a separate retcon.
