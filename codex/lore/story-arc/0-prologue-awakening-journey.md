# 0 - Prologue: Awakening Journey

## Scope
Establish the player baseline, core controls, first companion bond, and transition into the main campaign loop.

## Level Band
- Levels: 1-9
- Rank State: Hidden E -> Revealed E

## Entry Conditions
- New character starts in unstable tutorial space.
- No prior divine positioning.

## Structure Beats
1. Basic onboarding: movement, interaction, combat fundamentals.
2. Companion onboarding: initial Whisper bond and command basics.
3. Social onboarding: first ally group contact and trust checkpoint.
4. Manifestation checkpoint: player form stabilizes and campaign identity is set.
5. Departure checkpoint: player exits tutorial state into Act 1.

## Required Outputs
- Character is fully manifested and playable in campaign context.
- Starter companion state is active.
- Core systems unlocked for divine trials and progression.

## Exit Gate
- Prologue completion flag set.
- Route to Act 1 unlocked.
