# Critical Area Scaling

Critical area scaling expands the area covered by a critical area-of-effect event.

## Scaling

Criticality can increase an AoE's reach or coverage. This changes which targets can be reached, not the damage multiplier applied to a hit target.

## Scope

- Applies to area-of-effect events that qualify as critical.
- Damage to each target uses the applicable damage multiplier and normal defenses.
- The exact area measurement and critical resolution policy are not defined here.
