# Critical Ailment Scaling

Critical ailment scaling strengthens the effect of an ailment applied by a critical result.

## Scaling

Criticality scales the ailment's effect, such as its intensity or duration, according to the ailment's rules. The critical damage multiplier separately scales damage dealt by damaging ailments.

## Scope

- Applies to ailments when their application qualifies as critical, including damaging and non-damaging ailments.
- Does not replace the ailment's normal damage type or the target's applicable defenses.
- Does not itself define critical chance or ailment-specific scaling values.
