# Critical Damage Multiplier

The critical damage multiplier scales the damage dealt by a critical damage instance.

## Scaling

The multiplier is the damage-scaling component of criticality. It affects damage magnitude without changing critical chance, area coverage, or ailment behavior.

## Scope

- Applies to direct damage from a critical hit or effect.
- Uses the damage type and defenses that normally apply to the damage instance.
- Does not, by itself, change the number of hits or targets.
